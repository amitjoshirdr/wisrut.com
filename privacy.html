Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wisrut.com (“Wisrut”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you use our website and tools such as Margo – Student News Brief and Margo Search. We are based in India and our primary audience is students.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following types of information when you use our site:

  • Technical information – such as your IP address, browser type, approximate location, device type and pages visited. This is collected automatically by our hosting provider and analytics tools.
  • Usage information – such as prompts you type into Margo, buttons you click and features you use. This helps us improve the experience for students.
  • Cookies and similar technologies – we and our partners (including Google AdSense) may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences and show relevant advertisements.
  • Voluntary information – if you contact us by email or any contact form, we will receive the information you choose to share (such as your name, email address and message).

2. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate and maintain Wisrut.com and our tools like Margo.
  • Improve content quality, prompts and user experience for students.
  • Detect, prevent and address technical issues or misuse.
  • Show advertisements in a way that complies with Google and local policies.
  • Respond to your questions, feedback or support requests.

3. Google AdSense and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display ads on certain pages (for example, Margo News Brief and Margo Search). Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, we use a consent management platform (CMP) to collect your ad consent choices as required by Google. You can review or change these choices using the consent banner or settings when it is shown.

You can learn more about how Google uses data from its partners here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites .

4. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and local storage (for example, in Margo we store your prompts and article history in your browser) to make the service faster and more convenient. You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings, or by using the “Hard reset” options inside Margo where available.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect some features of the site.

5. Children and students

Wisrut is designed for students and educators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the age permitted by local law without parental consent. Students should avoid entering sensitive personal information (such as full home address, financial details or medical information) into prompts or contact forms.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Service providers – such as hosting providers and analytics or advertising partners (including Google) who help us run the site.
  • Legal or security reasons – if required by law or to protect our rights, users or others.

7. Data storage and retention

Technical logs are typically stored by our hosting provider for a limited period and then deleted automatically. Content stored in your browser (localStorage, cookies) remains until you clear it or reset it from the Margo interface. We retain email or support communications for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain records.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete certain personal information, or to object to specific types of processing. You can also:

  • Clear your browser cookies and local storage related to Wisrut.com.
  • Use the CMP / consent banner (where shown) to manage your ad consent choices.
  • Contact us to request changes or deletion where possible.

9. Links to other sites

Our site may link to other websites (for example, reference articles, payment providers or educational resources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their policies separately.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes may also be announced on the site.
