About

I am Amit Joshi — founder of WisdomRoots, an early learning centre in Rudrapur, Uttarakhand, and a brand identity designer for education businesses. I hold a Masters in Digital Marketing from the University of Reading, UK, with electives in Business Intelligence and Marketing Analytics.

I run a preschool, so I see every day how parents actually decide whom to trust with their child. I bring that insight into every logo, prospectus and visual system I design — so your brand feels both beautiful and believable to the families you serve.

I design from research, not assumption. Before opening Figma, I study what your parents or learners are actually looking for. That evidence shapes every decision.
