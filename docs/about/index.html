About Wisrut

Wisrut is a free study site for college students in India.

How the notes are made

  • Notes are written or chosen by teachers, in their own words.
  • Every note shows its author and the date it was last updated.
  • Each note links to the official syllabus where one is available.
  • Files stay on the teacher’s own storage. Wisrut does not host them.
  • AI-written text is labelled and can be wrong.

Margo

Margo is the set of AI tools on Wisrut: current affairs and search.
